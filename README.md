# The Moon Finally Answers Back

One line, 174 characters, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the
wishes, howls and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

`main.py` (program/v1, Python 3, no network, no dependencies) writes the
entry deterministically to `/output/entry.txt`:

> You wished, you howled, you promised you'd change at 2 a.m. — four billion years of it. Tonight I finally answer: no. Ask the sun. He's younger, and he still believes in you.

The Moon answers directly, in second person, claims the sentence with "I",
and lands the comeback twice before the line ends: a flat "no", then a
deflection to the sun — younger, and still naive enough to believe in the
wishers. Wry, old, and tired of being wished upon, in one read.

Run it with `python3 main.py`. The output is identical on every run
(sha256 `0b998e543aa3fc419eb7113f32d1e5270f6759b05db8eaff474e848bca2d6347`).
